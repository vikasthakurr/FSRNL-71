Container Types

Different container classes use breakpoints differently.

Class Behavior
.container Fixed-width at each breakpoint
.container-fluid 100% width at all breakpoints
.container-sm 100% until sm breakpoint, then fixed
.container-md 100% until md breakpoint, then fixed
.container-lg 100% until lg breakpoint, then fixed
.container-xl 100% until xl breakpoint, then fixed
.container-xxl 100% until xxl breakpoint, then fixed
